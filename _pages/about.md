I enjoy building software tools that make life easier. My work connects building and scaling systems with understanding where trust can break down — especially when AI agents interact with software dependencies and the outside world.

My research spans the security of the Python ecosystem, membership inference attacks on neural networks, and conversational agents for group decision-making. In 2026, Pushkar Saraf and I published a [preprint on weaponizing setup instructions against AI coding agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15143). My [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=OP-8ZWAAAAAJ&hl=en) has the publication record; I share research and updates on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aadesh-bagmar/).

### Before Microsoft

I interned at [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/) with the Customer Trust team in summer 2020. Before that, I was a Senior Software Engineer at [Soroco](https://www.soroco.com/), where I helped build their process discovery platform.

I received my Master's degree in Computer Science (4.0 GPA) from the [University of Maryland](https://www.cs.umd.edu/) in 2021, working as a Research Assistant with [Prof. Jim Purtilo](https://seam.cs.umd.edu/purtilo/) in the [SEAM lab](https://seam.cs.umd.edu/team). My undergraduate degree is in Computer Science from [NIT Surat](https://www.svnit.ac.in/web/department/computer/btechnew.php) (2016), where I worked on Computer Vision with [Prof. M.A. Zaveri](https://svnit.ac.in/maz/).
